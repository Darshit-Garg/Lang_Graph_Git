ACADEMIC & EXAMINATION REGULATIONS 2026:
1. Attendance Requirement: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% overall attendance to be eligible to sit for end-semester examinations.
2. Makeup Exams: Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to medical reasons or official college representations may apply for a makeup exam with the Dean of Academics.
3. Placement Eligibility: Students eligible for campus placements must have no active backlogs and a minimum CGPA of 6.5.
4. Library Rules: Up to 4 books can be borrowed at a time for a duration of 14 days. Re-issuance is allowed once.
